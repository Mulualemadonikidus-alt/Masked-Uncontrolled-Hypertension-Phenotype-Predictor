Clinical disclaimer: This tool is intended for research and clinical decision support in resource-limited settings. It is based on a binary logistic regression model derived from a cross-sectional Ethiopian cohort (n=244; MUCH n=75, SCH n=54) using ISH 2020 thresholds. Results should be interpreted by a qualified clinician alongside the full clinical picture. External validation in independent LMIC cohorts is recommended before implementation as a standalone triage tool. Not a substitute for ambulatory blood pressure monitoring (ABPM).
